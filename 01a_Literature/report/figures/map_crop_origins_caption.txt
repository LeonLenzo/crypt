Distribution of Field Cereal RNA-seq Studies

FULL CAPTION

Geographic, compositional and temporal distribution of the field cereal RNA-seq
cohort: 7,666 publicly archived samples from 41 BioProjects, each curated to a
field setting against a quoted sentence in an available manuscript. Colour marks
the crop throughout.

Map. One point per locality and crop, at coordinates geocoded from the locality
string; 7,342 samples at 406 localities in 39 countries are shown. Point area is
proportional to the LOGARITHM of the number of samples at that locality, so a
larger point indicates more samples but area is not proportional to count; exact
counts are given in the bars below. Points are semi-transparent, so overlapping
localities compound into darker patches. Country outlines are drawn in the
background colour.

Samples per crop (left) and distinct localities per crop (centre). The same
cohort counted two ways, sharing a category axis; the samples axis is reversed so
the two run outward from the crop labels. Localities are distinct locality
strings after geocoding, not sampling points, so repeated sampling of one site
across seasons counts once.

Collection year (right). Samples per year of collection, stacked by crop, over
2012 to 2024. Based on the 7,417 of 7,666 samples carrying a collection date.
Years with no samples are plotted as zero rather than omitted, so a gap in
collection appears as a gap.


WHAT IS NOT SHOWN

324 of the 7,666 samples (4.2%) are absent from the map but present in all three
lower panels. They are not a geocoding failure:

  311  carry a country and nothing finer, so there is no point to place them at.
       Mostly United Kingdom (82), France (40), South Africa (35), Denmark (20),
       Germany (17), Kenya (16), China (14) and Ireland (12). These are the rust
       surveys, where the submitter recorded the country of origin of an isolate
       and no collection site.
   13  have no location recorded at all: 12 from PRJNA1161467, whose paper gives
       only author affiliations, and 1 orphan run in PRJEB39201.

Three crops below a 100-sample floor are excluded from every panel: barley (3
samples), and rye, triticale, oat and the millets together (53). Barley's
absence is a property of the archive, not of this figure - there is no field
barley study in the cohort, only incidental barley samples inside wheat rust
surveys.

Localities are placed at three precisions, recorded per locality in
data/gold/cereal_localities.tsv: 3,486 samples at a named locality, 3,907 at a
locality whose research-facility name had to be dropped before it would resolve
(for example "USA: Nebraska, Lincoln (UNL Havelock Research Farm)" placed as
Lincoln, Nebraska), and 3 at province level.


SHORT CAPTION

Distribution of 7,666 field cereal RNA-seq samples from 41 BioProjects, coloured
by crop. Map: one point per locality and crop, point area proportional to log
sample count; 7,342 samples at 406 localities in 39 countries, the remaining 324
carrying no locality finer than a country. Left and centre: the same cohort as
samples and as distinct localities per crop. Right: samples per year of
collection, stacked by crop, for the 7,417 samples carrying a date.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT THIS FIGURE SHOWS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A paired horizontal bar chart showing the absolute number of confirmed
BioSamples per plant host species, split by whether the sample had a single
pathogen detected or a co-infection. Top 20 hosts by total co-infected
BioSamples are shown. Both modes (MAL + HAL) combined.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DESIGN
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Unit: biosample-representative BioSamples (one row per biological sample).
Host species: resolved from STAT Viridiplantae leaf species (host column in
runs.tsv), truncated to binomial (first two words). Host must have ≥ 10
confirmed BioSamples to appear (MIN_BS = 10).
Bars: left bar = single infection (green #27ae60), right bar = co-infection
(orange #e67e22). X axis: log10 scale, 1–10k range.
Host family: joined from crypt_host_tree_meta.tsv for context.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
INTERPRETATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Hosts with a large co-infected bar relative to single indicate high field
  co-infection prevalence for that species.
- The log scale means a similar visual gap between bars represents a 10×
  difference in counts — check the actual numbers (printed on bar ends).
- Wheat (Triticum aestivum), rice (Oryza sativa), and maize (Zea mays) tend
  to dominate as the most-studied crop hosts with the deepest SRA coverage.
- Co-infection count is a convolution of true biological prevalence and
  sequencing effort — hosts with more SRA runs will show more co-infections
  regardless of biology. Normalise by single-infection count (or total
  BioSamples) for a fair prevalence estimate across hosts.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CAVEATS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- "Co-infection" here includes all co_infection_flag values (multi_species +
  multi_kingdom). For a more conservative estimate, filter to
  same_genus_secondary=False (see study design figures for that breakdown).
- Host resolution depends on STAT Viridiplantae leaf assignment: runs where
  STAT only resolves to a broad clade (e.g. Poales) are excluded from the
  host-specific count and do not appear here.
- Both MAL and HAL modes included — a single BioSample can only appear once
  (biosample_representative deduplication applied across modes).

FULL CAPTION

Runs removed at each stage of the inclusion funnel, for the four cereal crops this study
covers. Literature Search is the curated cohort. Naturally Infected is a methodological
filter, removing runs whose originating study did not provide an unmanipulated,
naturally infected field sample: disease exposure not determinable from the paper,
fungicide protection, rain exclusion, inoculation rather than natural infection, or
plants not field-grown throughout. Library Depth is a technical filter, removing
libraries below 0.463 Gkmer, the count of k-mer start positions a library provides at k
= 35. Filter rows give runs removed; Total Remaining is the analysed set.

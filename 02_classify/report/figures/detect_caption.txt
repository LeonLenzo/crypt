FULL CAPTION

(a) THE DECISION SURFACE. All 300,061 species-rank candidates across 1,184 runs,
binned in log space by how much of a taxon the reads could have covered (redundancy)
against how much they did (coverage), both normalised by that taxon's own db_v4
minimizer total so taxa of any genome size share one scale. Shading is the fraction of
each bin that was CALLED, so the boundary shows as a gradient rather than a line.

Two boundaries are drawn. The horizontal is the abundance floor (coverage >= 1%): is
the organism's DNA there at all. The curve is the shape test (saturation >= 0.05, i.e.
coverage >= 0.05 x (1-exp(-redundancy))): are the reads spread across the taxon's k-mer
space or piled onto a few loci. They answer different questions and are not
interchangeable -- that distinction is carried over from the db_v3 filter, where score
alone gave wheat 80% co-infected by admitting trace detections and the coverage floor
brought it to 47%.

  candidates                         300,061
  rejected, below absolute floor            281,291
  rejected, below coverage floor              8,448
  rejected, reads piled, not spread             460
  CALLED                               9,862

The third floor cannot be drawn here: an absolute 10,000 distinct minimizers is a
different line for every taxon once normalised, so it does not project onto these axes.
It is not optional -- 1% of db_v4's smallest species is 29 k-mers, reachable by
cross-mapping alone, and 20 species have ceilings under 100,000.

(b) PATHOGENS PER SAMPLE, by crop and by sampling stratum. Plant-pathogen status is a
LABEL from holobase applied AFTER detection, never a filter before it: the db_v3 filter
skipped any taxon outside PHI-base's 632, which under db_v4 would discard every
resident the database was expanded to hold.

  >=2 plant pathogens: 701 of 1,184 runs (59%)

(c) WHAT IS ACTUALLY BEING CALLED, by number of runs, coloured by FUNGuild guild. A
species carrying several guild strings is drawn under the strongest claim (pathogen
before endophyte before saprotroph); the full string stays in _classified.tsv.

WHAT THIS IS NOT. These are not prevalence estimates. Every run classified so far is
STAT-retained, because the reads on disk were downloaded through the STAT run table,
which selected for pathogen presence: projects where STAT kept <50% of runs give 83%
co-infection against 58% where it kept >=90%. The thresholds are also a provisional
operating point, as the db_v3 weights were -- the sensitivity grid spans 3,396 to
17,755 calls across plausible floors, and calibrating the saturation floor needs the
axenic controls.

FULL CAPTION

All 1,068 classified wheat libraries, from 12 BioProjects across 41 countries. Each
project declared exactly one target pathogen, and the declared species differs between
projects, which turns the comparison below into a test rather than an observation.

PANEL a. THE SAME SPECIES, DECLARED AND UNDECLARED. For each species that some project
declared, the detection rate in libraries whose study declared it, against libraries whose
study did not. Same database, same thresholds; the only difference is what the paper said.

  species                    declared        undeclared
  Fusarium graminearum       50% (n=  12)      0% (n=1056)
  Puccinia graminis         100% (n=  67)      3% (n=1001)
  Puccinia striiformis      100% (n= 890)     15% (n= 178)
  Puccinia triticina        100% (n=  99)     19% (n= 969)

Overall the declared pathogen is recovered in 1,061 of 1,068 libraries (99.3%). Cross-mapping
cannot produce that asymmetry, because cross-mapping does not know what the paper said.
The same comparison answers the congener objection for the rusts: if Puccinia calls were
smearing across congeners, every project would show all three rusts, and PRJNA256347
reports 100% striiformis with 0% triticina and 0% graminis across 35 libraries.

PANEL b. Zymoseptoria tritici detection rate by country, one point per BioProject, for
countries with at least 8 libraries and cells with at least 6. Agreement between points in
a row is agreement between independent studies that share nothing but the country. The
gradient reproduces the known distribution of septoria tritici blotch, which is the
dominant wheat disease of maritime northwest Europe and minor in drier and warmer wheat
regions, and the pipeline was given no geographic information of any kind.

THE SEVEN NON-DETECTIONS ARE NOT BORDERLINE. Six of the seven are PRJNA1181034, a field
Fusarium graminearum spike trial whose 12 libraries split bimodally: six at 14.8 to 29.3%
coverage and six at 0.00 to 0.11%, which is 113 to 3,675 distinct minimizers against
491,872 to 974,167. Two to four orders of magnitude is not a threshold being brushed, and
the reading is uninfected or mock controls inside the trial. The absolute floor separated
infected from uninfected cleanly in the one project that contained both.

DEPTH does not explain the spatial pattern: across the countries with at least 10
libraries, the correlation between a country's median G_kmer and its co-infection rate is
r = -0.053 over 18 countries. Depth spans a factor of 300 across the wider cohort and
covaries with crop, which is why this work makes no comparison between crops.

TIME IS NOT SHOWN. Pooled by collection year the rate rises monotonically and it is a
composition artefact: the countries sampled change with the year, and 14 of the 17
libraries collected in 2018 come from two Argentinian sites. Holding country constant it
largely dissolves. geography_year.tsv carries the table.

WHAT THIS IS NOT. Every library here was collected because the plant was diseased, so the
denominator is conditioned on disease. The quantity measured is the rate at which a SECOND
pathogen accompanies a known one, not prevalence.

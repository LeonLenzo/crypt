FULL CAPTION

Runs surviving each of the four inclusion gates, applied in the order shown, by crop.
Gates are NESTED, not independent: every run is attributed to the FIRST gate it fails,
so a run excluded for disease exposure is not counted again at the depth floor. The
curated cohort of 7,688 runs across 41 BioProjects reduces to 3,680 runs across 27
BioProjects.

Each gate falls on the crops unevenly, and that is the result the table carries. All 490
fungicide-protected and rain-excluded runs are rice, as are all 104 runs not grown in
the field throughout; all 43 inoculated runs are maize or wheat. The depth floor is the
only gate that touches four crops.

RICE LEAVES THE ANALYSIS ENTIRELY: 0 of 2,784 rice runs are retained. 594 are excluded
by a documented feature of the study design (fungicide application, rain exclusion, or
not field-grown throughout), 633 are recorded as disease exposure undetermined, and
1,557 fall below the depth floor.

The depth exclusion does not depend on where the floor is set. Those runs have a median
0.019 Gkmer and a maximum of 0.214 against a floor of 0.463, and none is within 2x of
it, so any floor at or above 0.22 Gkmer excludes exactly the same runs. Rice is
therefore reported as outside the analysable set rather than as a crop in which no co-
infection was found.

Depth is Gkmer, the number of k-mer start positions a library provides at k = 35, not
reads or bases. ENA read_len is bases per spot, so a paired library's per-read length is
half of it, and at k = 35 a 51 nt read yields 17 k-mers against 116 for a 150 nt read.

Retained by crop: Maize 1,374, Wheat 1,132, Sorghum 1,118, Other cereal 54, Barley 2.

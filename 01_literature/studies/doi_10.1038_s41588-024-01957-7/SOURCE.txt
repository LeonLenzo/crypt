Checked, all of it:
  - archive: collection_date is the literal string "missing" on all 229 runs; dev_stage gives
    "18 days after pollination", a developmental stage, not a calendar date
  - main text Methods: accessions were "planted in Wuhan, Yangluo, Qinghai and Yangzhou, in
    the six growth seasons (2012-2018)" and never assigns a season to the silique RNA-seq
  - Supplementary Methods (MOESM1, "RNA extraction and library preparation of RNA-Seq"):
    "Shoot apical meristem (SAM), young leaves and developing siliques and seeds at 18 days
    after pollination (DAP) were collected for RNA-Seq. Each sample was immediately flash
    frozen in liquid nitrogen; each sequenced sample comprised a mixture of four plants from
    each accession." Stage and pooling, no year. The word Yangluo does not appear in MOESM1.
  - MOESM3, all 23 supplementary tables: no year, season or date column anywhere. Table S1 is
    the accession panel keyed on Sample ID (GDRP001...), which matches our SampleName
    (Silique_GDRP003), but its only place column is "Origin" = germplasm country of origin
    (China, Australia, ...), NOT a growing site. Do not mistake it for one.

So these 229 are the cohort's only undated samples, and that is a property of the published
record rather than of our curation. A year could only come from the authors or from the
Chinese GSA deposit (PRJCA013095).

NCBI BioProjects, so the seed arms are presumably in PRJCA013095 at the NGDC.

*** A PHRASE THAT WOULD FOOL A KEYWORD OR LLM CLASSIFIER ***
The abstract says the tissues were examined "under NATURALLY OCCURRING WATER-LIMITED
CONDITIONS", and the introduction contrasts the work with PEG studies because PEG "is
different from gradually occurring natural drought conditions in the field". Both phrases
describe the DROUGHT METHOD - withholding water rather than osmotic shock - and neither says
anything about the setting, which is pots in a Beijing greenhouse. Read only the abstract and
this project classifies as field. This is the same hazard that made llm_study_setting call a
UC Davis inoculation atlas "field" (see crypt-sample-provenance-unverified).

Design: 4 cultivars x 3 tissues (root/stem/leaf, 48 runs each) x 2 treatments (control,
drought) x 3 replicates = 72 BioSamples, each deposited as 2 runs. 48 of the 144 runs are
ROOT and would fail the aerial-tissue rule even if the setting qualified.

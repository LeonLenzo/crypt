FULL CAPTION

The cohort redrawn so the outer ring carries the study's own declaration: 28 papers,
31 BioProjects and 1,560 runs, at the positions network.png uses. Nothing has moved
between the two figures.

Each BioProject is filled by the pathogen the generating paper named, shaped by
whether the deposit holds any field material (circle) or is lab only (square), and
outlined heavily where it holds field wheat. Its runs surround it: green for field-
collected Triticum, dark for lab material, hollow for field material off-host or not
RNA-seq.

FIELD AND LAB ARE MARKED AT BOTH LEVELS, because neither level can carry it alone. 4
deposits hold both kinds, so a square on them would be false and a circle alone says
nothing about the lab runs inside; a circle means the deposit HOLDS field material,
never that all of it is field. But 17 of the 86 lab runs in the cohort sit inside a
drawn cloud and the other 69 are in the 4 lab-only deposits, whose clouds are not
drawn, so the run mark alone would show a fifth of the lab arm and silently drop the
rest.

THE DECLARATION IS WHAT THE STUDY SAID, NOT WHAT IS IN THE READS. It is curated once
per BioProject from the paper that generated it, against a quoted sentence
(data/curation/declared_pathogens.tsv). It is not a detection and the two must not be
read as the same thing: the chapter's question is precisely what the reads hold that
the declaration does not.

15 of 31 deposits declare Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici. That is the cohort, not
a sampling choice: public wheat RNA-seq is overwhelmingly a yellow-rust archive, and
it is why a pooled rate over these runs is a statement about surveillance effort.

The 9 deposits that declare no fungus are virome surveys. They are the only part of
the cohort that did not condition on fungal disease, so they are drawn as their own
colour rather than as a missing value.

4 deposits are lab only (PRJEB12497, PRJNA396589, PRJNA176472, PRJNA749614), holding
haustoria, infected tissue and spores. 6 deposits carry no sample cloud because they
hold no field wheat, and 2 of those are field surveys of another host: a distinction
the plain figure cannot draw, because there both are a hollow circle.

1,396 of 1,560 runs are field wheat; 1,435 runs are drawn inside a cloud.

Reanalysis edges, as in network.png. Edges are primary attribution only: 32 of them,

Node positions carry no meaning. Cloud area tracks run count through the packing but

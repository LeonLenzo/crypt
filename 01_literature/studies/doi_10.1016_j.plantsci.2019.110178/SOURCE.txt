THE SPLIT IS ONE SENTENCE: "Day 0 samples were field-collected." Everything after Day 0 is a
forcing time course indoors: "Dormant canes were collected ... in mid-winter [February 2015;
1030 chill units] ... The cuttings were randomized and placed with cut ends in cups of water
and placed within a growth room with forcing conditions: 22 C and 16/8 h light/dark." Buds
were then sampled DAILY until budbreak, to day 10-17 depending on genotype.

So 17 Day-0 runs are field and the other 172 are growth chamber (forcing). Without the paper
this project looks like 189 field dormant-bud runs; the archive gives no hint either way.

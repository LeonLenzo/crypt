FULL CAPTION

Geographic, compositional and temporal distribution of the field cereal RNA-seq
cohort: 7,666 publicly archived samples from 41 BioProjects, each curated to a
field setting against a quoted sentence in an available manuscript. Colour marks
the crop throughout.

(a) Map. One point per locality and crop. Point area is proportional to the
LOGARITHM of the number of samples there, so a larger point means more samples
but area is not proportional to count; exact counts are in the bars below.
Points are semi-transparent, so overlapping localities compound into darker
patches. Country outlines are drawn in the background colour.

  Circles, marked "Locality" in the Resolution key (7,342 samples at 406
  localities), sit at coordinates geocoded from the locality string recorded for
  the sample.

  Diamonds, marked "Country" (311 samples across 24 countries), sit at the
  centroid of the largest landmass polygon of their country. These samples name
  a country and nothing finer, so the diamond states WHICH COUNTRY and is drawn
  at a place nobody sampled. It is not a collection site and should not be read
  as one.

(b) Samples per crop and (c) distinct localities per crop. The same cohort
counted two ways. Axis values are scaled by the factor given in the axis title,
so a label of 3 in (b) is 3,000 samples and a label of 3 in (c) is 300
localities. Localities are distinct locality strings finer than a country,
so bare-country samples add to (b) and not to (c), and repeated sampling of one
site across seasons counts once.

(d) Year. Samples per year of collection, stacked by crop, over 2012 to 2024.
Only the first and last year are labelled, to give the range; every year in
between has its own bar. Based on the 7,417 of 7,666 samples carrying a
collection date. Bars rather than a continuous series, because year here is a
discrete count: an area or line would draw a slope between 2015 and 2016
asserting collection at every instant in between, and none occurred. Every year
in the range has a position on the axis, so a year with no collection reads as
an absent bar rather than being skipped.


WHAT IS NOT SHOWN

13 of the 7,666 samples carry no location at all and are absent from the map
while remaining in the other three panels: 12 from PRJNA1161467, whose paper
gives only author affiliations, and 1 orphan run in PRJEB39201.

The 311 samples drawn at country centroids are not a geocoding failure. They are
rust surveys, where the submitter recorded the country an isolate came from and
no collection site, and ENA holds no latitude, longitude or locality field for
them. Where a finer value existed in a supplement it was already imported: the
Ada21 projects (PRJEB31334, PRJEB39201, PRJEB15280, PRJEB33109, PRJNA256347) and
PRJEB36485 take their location from supplementary tables that themselves give
only a country for these samples. One lead remains open: Lewis et al. 2024
(10.1111/nph.19864) cites "Dataset S2, All Puccinia graminis isolates used in
this study" as a separate .xlsx that has not been obtained, and it may carry
collection sites for PRJEB65589's 53 country-only samples.

Three crops below a 100-sample floor are excluded from every panel: barley (3
samples), and rye, triticale, oat and the millets together (53). Barley's
absence is a property of the archive, not of this figure - there is no field
barley study in the cohort, only incidental barley samples inside wheat rust
surveys.

Geocoded localities are placed at three precisions, recorded per locality in
data/gold/cereal_localities.tsv: 296 localities resolved as given, 108 only
after a research-facility name was dropped (for example "USA: Nebraska, Lincoln
(UNL Havelock Research Farm)" placed as Lincoln, Nebraska), and 3 at province
level.


SHORT CAPTION

Distribution of 7,666 field cereal RNA-seq samples from 41 BioProjects, coloured
by crop. (a) One point per locality and crop, point area proportional to log
sample count. Circles are 7,342 samples at 406 geocoded localities; diamonds are
311 samples that name only a country, drawn at its centroid and not at a
collection site; 13 samples carry no location and are absent. (b, c) The same cohort as
samples, and as distinct localities finer than a country. (d) Samples
per year of collection, stacked by crop, for the 7,417 samples carrying a date.

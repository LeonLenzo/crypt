FULL CAPTION

Taxonomic distribution of the 1,172 reference taxa that have both a USDA
record and a gene model, drawn as a circular cladogram built from the NCBI
taxonomy. IT IS A TAXONOMY, NOT A PHYLOGENY: radius encodes rank, from kingdom
at the centre to species at the rim, and carries no information about
divergence. Branch lengths are not evolutionary distances and must not be read
as any.

Only the observation rows of panel A appear. Congeners are excluded, because a
taxon admitted so the classifier has a competitor is never reported as a
detection, and taxa with no gene model are excluded because they contribute no
CDS. These 1,172 are the taxa the database is actually built from.

Bar length is the number of genome assemblies for that taxon on a log scale,
with dotted gridlines and numerals at 1, 10 and 100. The spread is wide: 808
of 1,172 taxa have a single assembly and the largest has 435.

Bar colour is the taxon's cell in panel A, using the same five colours, so a
hue means the same thing in both panels:

  wheat pathogen                                24
  pathogen elsewhere, recorded on wheat         21
  non-pathogen, recorded on wheat              108
  pathogen elsewhere, not on wheat              46
  non-pathogen, not on wheat                   973

The inner ring at the base of each bar is the share of that taxon's assemblies
carrying a gene model: pale where all of them do, dark purple where few do.
620 of 1,172 taxa are fully annotated and 552 are not. The dark segments are
the gene-prediction worklist, because those are genomes already deposited
whose CDS the database cannot currently use.

The 5 arcs outside the bars mark the taxonomic classes large enough to label,
each identified by a SYMBOL rather than a name: circle Agaricomycetes (202
taxa); square Dothideomycetes (160 taxa); diamond Eurotiomycetes (129 taxa);
triangle Leotiomycetes (74 taxa); inverted triangle Sordariomycetes (366
taxa). A symbol is used because a class name set radially reads at a different
angle in every quadrant and competes with the bars it annotates. The arcs are
reading aids for position in the tree, not a result, and the smaller classes
are present as tips but carry no arc.


HOW TO READ IT

The panel is mostly one pale colour on purpose. The reference is a community,
and the organisms anyone has studied are a sliver of it: the pathogens are the
few bright spikes against a pale field, and they are tallest where they are
hyper-sequenced, which is a statement about research attention and not about
abundance in a field.


WHAT IS NOT SHOWN

Nothing here is a detection. Bar length counts deposited assemblies, so a tall
bar means a well-sequenced organism, never an abundant or a frequently
detected one.

Taxa with no gene model are absent from this panel but remain in the database
scope and in panel A, where the gap between the Asm. and CDS rows measures
them.

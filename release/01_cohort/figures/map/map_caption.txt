FULL CAPTION

Collection origin of the field wheat cohort: 1,396 runs, of which 1,075 are drawn at a
named place and 289 at a country centroid.

Only field-collected Triticum is plotted. Lab material and off-host runs are excluded
even where they carry coordinates, because an isolate deposit records where its
pustule was collected, not where anything was sampled. The test is the same one that
colours a run green in the network figure, so the two panels describe the same
samples.

Green circles are 437 named places. Blue circles are 23 country centroids, carrying
the runs whose papers record a country and nothing finer; the largest are South Africa
65, USA 47, UK 33.

A BLUE CIRCLE IS NOT A COLLECTION SITE. It states WHICH COUNTRY and sits at the
centroid of that country's largest landmass polygon, a place nobody sampled.

Point diameter scales with the square root of the runs at that point, so area is
proportional to count and the key can be read off. Points are semi-transparent, so
overlapping places compound into darker patches. The largest named place is
Switzerland: Zurich with 30 runs; the key runs to 65 because the largest marks on the
map are blue, not green.

THE BIGGEST MARKS ARE THE LEAST PRECISE, which is the opposite of what a reader
expects. Country circles carry up to 65 runs and named places at most 30.

map_europe.{png,pdf} is the second image: Europe, North Africa and the Near East, 19W
to 38E and 27N to 58N, where 289 of the 460 points sit and where the world view is one
blot. It is cropped to its contents rather than to the region, and it is drawn to the
SAME size scale as the world map. Both are rendered at the size they are placed at, so
a mark of a given size means the same number of runs in both and the key serves both.


WHAT IS NOT SHOWN

32 runs name no place at all and are absent from the map while remaining in every
count elsewhere.

The 289 country-level runs are not a geocoding failure. Each was pursued to the
source: three supplementary tables were read end to end (Lewis et al. 2024 Dataset S2,
Hubbard et al. 2015, and Table S13 of the 2025 brown rust study, joined run by run
through ENA's secondary accessions), and each records a country and nothing finer for
exactly these samples. Where a finer value existed it was taken — five Western
Australian runs were placed at Kulin, Corrigin, Goomalling and Wongan Hills from Table
1 of 10.3390/v15020401, matched on the BioSample isolate field.

Named places are placed at several precisions, recorded per run in
curated_attributes.tsv: 874 locality, 56 uncurated, 43 locality-nofacility, 38
corrected-medium, 37 corrected-high, 16 corrected-low, 10 country, 1 region.

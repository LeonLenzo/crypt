The Sato 2024 Arabidopsis field cluster: eight BioProjects, one experiment, two papers.

PRJNA1055060 Zurich 2017 HiSeq2500    PRJNA1055734 Zurich 2017 HiSeqX
PRJNA1055104 Zurich 2018 HiSeq2500    PRJNA1056126 Zurich 2018 HiSeqX
PRJNA1055317 Otsu   2017 HiSeq2500    PRJNA1055736 Otsu   2017 HiSeqX
PRJNA1055424 Otsu   2018 HiSeq2500    PRJNA1055939 Otsu   2018 HiSeqX

4,796 runs = 2,398 libraries x 2 platforms. Submitter: University of Zurich.

The RNA-Seq has NO paper of its own. Evidence comes from two sources:

  Sato Y, Shimizu-Inatsugi R, Takeda K, Schmid B, Nagano AJ, Shimizu KK. 2024.
  "Reducing herbivory in mixed planting by genomic prediction of neighbor effects in the
  field." Nature Communications 15:8482.  doi:10.1038/s41467-024-52374-7  PMC11458863
  Open access. Fetched from Europe PMC 2026-10-06.
    Supplies: the FIELD EXPERIMENT. Both sites with coordinates, the potting and transfer,
    the randomised block design, the herbivore surveys, the sampling window.
    Contains NO RNA-Seq section and deposits no sequence data. Its own deposits are
    phenotypes (github.com/yassato/AraHerbNeighborGen, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.7945317),
    flea-beetle COI sequences (OQ857829-OQ857834) and AraGWAS genotypes.

  Tomita A, Maeda T, Mori-Moriyama N, Nomura Y, Kurita Y, Kashima M, Betsuyaku S, Sato Y,
  Nagano AJ. "Transcriptomic biomarkers reveal jasmonic and salicylic acid state under
  field herbivory." bioRxiv doi:10.1101/2025.05.29.656841 -- VERSION 3 ONLY.
    https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.05.29.656841v3.full-text
    Supplies: the SEQUENCING. Leaf harvested three weeks post-transplantation within a
    two-hour window around solar noon at each site, Lasy-Seq v1.1, HiSeq2500 and HiSeqX
    giving 50-bp and 150-bp 3'-end reads respectively.
    CAUTION: v1 and v2 are titled "Systematic measurements of dose-dependent responses for
    combinatorial treatments of SA and JA..." and contain NO field arm at all. Only v3
    (2026-02-13) adds the field work and names these eight accessions. Tom26 states it
    REUSED this data; its own deposit is PRJNA1232845 (768 in vitro SA/JA seedling samples).

Neither paper reports plant pathogens. Sato 2024 concerns insect herbivores; Tom26 concerns
SA/JA signalling state. Any fungus or oomycete in these reads is unreported by both.

sato24_runs.py derives the per-run table and the deduplication from runs.tsv plus the
project-to-site mapping in its PROJECTS dict (taken from the NCBI project titles, each of
which names its site, year and instrument).

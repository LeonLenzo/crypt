How many wheat pathogens one run carries, summarised per study. Each bar is
one BioProject and its length is its analysed runs divided into the share
carrying no wheat pathogen, one, two, three, and 4+; the darker the right-hand
end of a bar, the more of that study's samples held more than one pathogen at
once. The same 1,347 field-collected Triticum aestivum runs as the panel
above, over 22 BioProjects, ordered by the share of runs carrying two or more.
The number at the right of each row is that project's analysed runs, and the 3
projects with fewer than 10 sit in a block of their own at the foot of the
panel, because a rate over four or eight runs is not a rate.

Across the cohort 94 runs (7.0%) carry 0, 716 runs (53.2%) carry 1, 374 runs
(27.8%) carry 2, 120 runs (8.9%) carry 3, 43 runs (3.2%) carry 4+, so 537 runs
(39.9%) carry two or more and the most crowded run holds 6. THE POOLED FIGURE
IS NOT THE FINDING: across the 19 projects with at least 10 runs the rate runs
from 0.0% to 84.6%, and the spread between studies is larger than anything a
single number over the cohort could report. 14 wheat pathogens contribute
2,006 calls, led by Puccinia striiformis (1,029), Zymoseptoria tritici (299),
Puccinia triticina (282).

THE PROJECT IS THE UNIT HERE, and in the panel above it is the run. That is
deliberate: what varies between studies is what they sampled and where, so
each project is one observation of its own rate and the panel draws the spread
rather than averaging it away. A pairwise co-occurrence view of the same calls
is NOT drawn, having been measured and rejected: conditioning on BioProject
reverses three of the eight commonest pathogen pairs, Puccinia striiformis
with P. triticina going from a marginal odds ratio of 0.28 to 4.43 within
projects, so an association drawn over runs would report where the surveys
sampled.

THE SECOND COLUMN IS THE OBVIOUS OBJECTION, TESTED. A study with few
co-infections might simply have shallow libraries, so each point is that
project's median fungal reads per run, the depth that remains once the host is
removed, on a log axis spanning three orders of magnitude. The rank
correlation with the co-infection rate is +0.23 over 22 projects, and 3 of the
7 projects with no co-infection at all sit at or above the cohort's median
depth (PRJNA1250571, PRJNA725323, PRJEB33109). Depth is therefore not what
separates the top of the panel from the bottom.

ACCESSIONS IN GREY are virus surveys, which declared no fungus because they
were not looking for one; 4 of the 7 projects with no co-infection are among
them, and 5 of the 22 projects in total. The marker is read from the curated
study_target column of supp_data_s2_cohort.tsv, never inferred from a study
having declared nothing.

Counting follows supp_data_s6_runs.tsv, one row per run, and counts every
wheat pathogen called in the arm: 14 taxa, where the panel above draws 13 rows
because Alternaria alternata is shown inside the Alternaria genus row. That
difference moves exactly one run from three pathogens to two and no run across
the two-or-more boundary. A call is presence, not infection; release/04_align
confirms each one read by read.

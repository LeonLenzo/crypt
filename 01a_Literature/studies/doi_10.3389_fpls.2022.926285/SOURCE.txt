FOUR BIOPROJECTS, FOUR RELATIONSHIPS. Data availability names PRJNA609211, PRJNA260762,
PRJNA825139 and PRJNA306542. Only the first and third are this group's own; PRJNA306542 and
PRJNA260762 are reanalysed (see crypt-umbrella-bioprojects).

At least three sequencing arms:
  1. growth-chamber hormone and abiotic-stress panel on Nipponbare. "cultured in the nutrient
     solution proposed by Yoshida et al. (1976) in a growth chamber (16 h light at 30 C and
     8 h dark at 25 C) for 15 days after germination", then PEG6000 / H2O2 / NaCl / antimycin
     A and ABA / GA4 / IAA / CTK / ethephon.
  2. the Baihe field experiment, 2016 (May to October), paired fields - a drought DT field
     from July 5 to August 15 and a drip-irrigated CK control - transplanted 15 June.
  3. CRISPR-Cas9 knockouts ppr035-1,2,3 and ppr406-1,2,3 plus WT, bred to T3 "for further
     field and laboratory experiments".

TREAT_SETTING_BAR — Co-infection rate by study type and setting
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
File: treat_setting_bar.pdf/.png

Two-panel faceted bar chart (grouped bars per LLM treatment category):

Treatment categories (x axis):
  single          — single pathogen / host response study
  host_study      — pure host biology (development, assembly, physiology)
  abiotic_stress  — drought, heat, salt, cold — no declared pathogen
  surveillance    — environmental/field monitoring without specific hypothesis
  coinf_experiment — intentional co-infection design

Setting (bar colour):
  Field     (#27ae60) — llm_study_setting = "field"
  Lab       (#8e44ad) — llm_study_setting = "lab"
  Unclear   (#b0b0b0) — llm_study_setting = "unclear"

Top panel: all co-infections (co_infection_flag ≠ "single")
Bottom panel: high-confidence only (same_genus_secondary = False)

Cells with < 5 BioProjects excluded. Y axis: co-infection rate (proportion
of biosample-rep BioSamples with co-infection detected in that cell).

Key finding: field single-pathogen BioSamples show substantially higher
co-infection rates than lab single-pathogen BioSamples. The HC filter
(bottom panel) collapses the lab rate toward zero while field remains
elevated, suggesting the field signal is not explained by same-genus k-mer
ambiguity. surveillance category shows high field co-infection rate,
consistent with environmental monitoring capturing real mixed infections.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEY MESSAGES FOR DISCUSSION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Field vs lab: field single-pathogen studies have 2–6× higher co-infection
   rate than lab studies. This is the core empirical finding supporting the
   "cryptic co-infection is common in field samples" hypothesis.
2. HC filter collapses lab rate: after removing same-genus secondaries, lab
   rate drops to ~2%, while field remains ~12%. This strongly suggests that
   most lab co-infection detections are k-mer ambiguity or contamination,
   while field detections contain genuine multi-organism signal.
3. surveillance and coinf_experiment categories: not directly comparable to
   single-pathogen studies but shown for completeness. coinf_experiment BPs
   are intentional co-infection designs and should be excluded from
   prevalence estimates.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CAVEATS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- LLM classification (gpt-4o-mini) has 60% agreement with keyword classifier
  for treatment, 93% for setting. Disagreements are common in the single vs
  host_study boundary (~51% of KW host_study BPs also contain pathogen
  language). LLM output is more reliable than keyword-only.
- BioProject is the unit of classification; within a BioProject all BioSamples
  share the same treatment/setting label regardless of individual sample
  variation. Mixed BioProjects (field + lab samples in the same project) are
  assigned a single dominant label.
- STAT Dikarya stall may suppress field co-infection rate for wheat rust
  studies in MAL mode (PST reads misclassified at 0% in STAT). The Kraken2
  pipeline will revise these estimates.

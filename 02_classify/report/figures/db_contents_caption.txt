Reference scope of db_v4. Circular cladogram of the 3,744 fungal species for which NCBI holds at least one assembly, drawn on the NCBI taxonomy held in holobase: topology is taxonomic, branch lengths are not evolutionary distance, and radius encodes taxonomic RANK (kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, species) rather than any measure of divergence. Tip colour gives the route that brought the species into scope, taking the strongest evidence where several apply (host (plant) 6, target 1,974, host associate 145, plant associate 1,137, congener 482); congeners are grey because they are present to give the classifier a competitor and are not reportable as detections. Each tip carries a radial bar on a log10 scale, pale for all 19,038 assemblies that exist and solid for the 4,569 that carry a gene model. Both bars start at the same origin, so the pale remainder is the log-fold gap between what exists and what a CDS-only database can use; the bars are not an additive stack. 1,599 species (43%) have at least one annotated assembly and can enter db_v4; the 2,145 entirely pale tips have assemblies but no gene model and are the gene-prediction worklist. Classes with at least 40 species are labelled.

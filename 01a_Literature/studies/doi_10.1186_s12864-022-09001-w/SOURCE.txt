Source for PRJNA753546, 151 runs: 148 maize/teosinte leaf (field) and 3 whole insect.

*** READ THIS BEFORE USING THESE RUNS: THE LIBRARIES ARE RIBODEPLETED, NOT polyA ***
"The DNA-free total RNA was subjected to ribosomal RNA depletion by Illumina Ribo-Zero rRNA
Removal Kit (Plant Leaf) ... Strand-specific cDNA libraries were prepared from ribosomal
depleted RNA using the NEBNext Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep kit."

Every other project in this cohort is polyA-selected. A ribodepleted library retains
non-polyadenylated and bacterial transcripts that polyA selection removes, so the MICROBIAL
FRACTION of reads is systematically higher here for reasons of chemistry, not biology.
Fungal mRNA is polyadenylated and is captured by both methods, so these runs are usable for
fungal detection - but any comparison expressed as a PERCENTAGE of classified reads is
invalid against the rest of the cohort. CLAUDE.md's existing rule, report read counts and not
percentages, is what makes these runs safe to include.

The archive's library_selection reads "cDNA" on all 151, which does not distinguish the two
chemistries. Only the paper does.

WHAT THIS FIGURE SHOWS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A five-column Sankey diagram tracing 11,117 BioSamples from the full pipeline
output through progressively finer classification layers, terminating at
co-infection status. "Unclear" nodes at each stage are terminal — only
BioSamples with classifiable metadata proceed to the next column.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
COLUMN STRUCTURE (left → right)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Col 0 — All BioSamples: 11,117 biosample-representative rows from
         biosample_kw.tsv (both modes, eukaryotic pathogens only, after
         9,002 deduplicated BioSamples from the euk-only upstream gate).

Col 1 — Evidence tier: does this BioProject have a publication or metadata?
  "With publication"  — primary_pmid present in literature.json
  "Metadata only"     — BioSample XML has geo_loc_name, tissue, or
                        collection_date but no linked publication
  "No evidence"       — neither; TERMINAL (grey, exits funnel)

Col 2 — Setting: LLM-classified study environment.
  "Field"             — llm_study_setting = "field"
  "Controlled"        — llm_study_setting = "lab"
  "Setting unclear"   — TERMINAL (grey, exits funnel)

Col 3 — Stress / treatment type:
  "Biotic"            — llm_treatment ∈ {single, coinf_experiment,
                        surveillance}  (pathogen-related study)
  "Abiotic"           — llm_treatment = "abiotic_stress"
  "No stress"         — llm_treatment = "host_study"
  "Design unclear"    — TERMINAL per setting (grey, exits funnel)

Col 4 — Co-infection outcome:
  "No co-infection"   (grey)   — co_infection_flag = "single"
  "Co-infected"       (orange) — co_infection_flag ≠ "single"
  Separate pairs for Field and Controlled.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEY MESSAGES FOR DISCUSSION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- The funnel shows how many BioSamples are classifiable at each stage:
  the "unclear" terminations reveal metadata gaps in SRA.
- Field biotic-stress BioSamples (dark green / biotic) reaching Col 4 with
  co-infection (orange) are the primary signal of interest — these are
  single-pathogen studies in field conditions where cryptic co-infection was
  detected without the original study reporting it.
- The controlled/biotic arm reaching co-infection is important context:
  lab studies with co-infection detections are more likely to reflect
  intentional co-infection experiments or k-mer ambiguity than true cryptic
  co-infection.
- Hover in the HTML version shows exact counts and percentages per flow.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CAVEATS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- BioProject-level LLM classification applied uniformly to all BioSamples in
  a project; within-project variation in setting or treatment is not captured.
- The evidence tier conflates publication quality (a preprint counts the same
  as a peer-reviewed paper).
- Co-infection includes all confidence levels (same_genus_secondary not
  filtered). Apply HC filter for conservative prevalence estimates.
- The Sankey is static once rendered; to change thresholds or classification
  logic, re-run sample_funnel.py.

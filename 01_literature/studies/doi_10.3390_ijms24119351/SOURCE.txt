*** WHY THIS COHORT IS FLAGGED sampling_selection = fungicide-treated ***

  "were provided Ortiva Top fungicide on 28 May and 15 June"
  "Soil around the plants was covered with a silicone cover to limit soil evaporation and
   was protected from rain using a cone-shaped polystyrene skirt."

Ortiva Top is azoxystrobin plus difenoconazole: broad-spectrum, systemic, applied twice,
with rain excluded from the pots. Fungal colonisation was actively suppressed.

On setting, tissue, location and date this looks like an ideal outdoor field cohort. For a
fungal co-infection question it is actively misleading, because a low or absent signal here
is partly an absence of OPPORTUNITY rather than an observation about field prevalence. That
is the opposite error to an inoculated cohort and just as disqualifying, which is why the
fourth sampling_selection value exists.

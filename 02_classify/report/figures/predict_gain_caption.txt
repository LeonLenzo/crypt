FULL CAPTION

CDS annotations available to db_v4 by route into scope. The SOLID bar is the 4,271
assemblies that carry an NCBI gene model and are therefore already in the database. The
PALE bar is the 3,930 assemblies belonging to species with NO gene model anywhere, which
are invisible to a CDS-only index today and are what ab-initio prediction would reach.
In species terms that is 1,547 species now and 2,138 more reachable.

Assemblies of species that ALREADY have an annotation are excluded from the pale bar.
They could be predicted too, but they add no new species, and the claim here is about
what becomes reachable rather than how many predictions could be run.
Routes, colours and the precedence rule are shared with the db_v4 ring figure; each
species is counted once, under the strongest evidence that applies.

  route                                           CDS now   addable     total
  pathogen recorded on wheat, rice, maize or sorghum     893       545     1,438
  plant pathogen, not recorded on those crops      1,444     1,548     2,992
  host associate: seen in a phytobiome study         244       106       350
  plant associate: recorded on a plant             1,117     1,256     2,373
  congener: shares a genus with a pathogen           537       475     1,012
  TOTAL                                            4,271     3,930     8,201

THE ADDITION IS NOT HYPOTHETICAL. Measured on 60 fungal assemblies that already carry
an NCBI gene model, so the annotation serves as ground truth, ab-initio prediction
recovers a median 96% of the true CDS k-mer space at 94% precision.
On 59 assemblies WITHOUT a gene model, a median 96% of the predicted k-mer
space is absent from db_v4 — so the pale bars are close to their full face value and
not largely redundant with what the database already holds.

THE host ROUTE IS NOT DRAWN. It is 6 plant host species (36 annotations) with ZERO
addable, so it
says nothing about additions while forcing a 180x axis range. Omitting it keeps the
chart linear, where a bar's LENGTH is its value; a log axis would break that for the
sake of one bar carrying no argument. The row stays in predict_gain.tsv.

WHAT THE BAR DOES NOT SAY. Annotations, not sequence: a pale bar counts assemblies that
would gain a gene model, not the k-mer space they contribute, and the two are not
proportional. Nor is every addable species equally worth adding — novelty against db_v4
ranges from 0.05 for species in densely sampled genera (Penicillium, Alternaria) to 0.99
for genera the database does not represent at all, which is where the phyllosphere
residents sit. Prediction quality also falls on large repeat-rich genomes, where
ab-initio calls genes inside transposons.

Wheat pathogens detected in 1,167 RNA-seq runs from 12 BioProjects, one column
per project and one row per pathogen. Cell shading is the share of that
project's runs in which the pathogen was called. A cell is drawn only where
the pathogen was called at least once, so blank means no run in that project
reached the detection floors. The outlined cell is the pathogen the study
itself declared.

The outlined cells are the positive control and they are read first. Across 13
declared study-pathogen pairs, the median pair has the declared organism in
100% of the project's runs, 10 of 13 pairs reach 100%, and the lowest is 27%
(P. triticina in PRJNA718488). No outlined cell is empty: there is no project
whose declared target the screen failed to find. Nothing was tuned per
project, and the same floors produced every other cell in the panel.

Everything off the outlines is undeclared. 13 pathogens are called in projects
that did not declare them, over 59 study-pathogen pairs and counting the rarer
taxa listed below that are not given a row, and 533 runs carry at least one.
The leading undeclared organism is Z. tritici, called in 292 runs across 8
projects and declared by none of them.

The result is the SPREAD across projects, not a pooled rate: the share of a
project's runs carrying any undeclared wheat pathogen runs from 0%
(PRJEB33109) to 88% (PRJEB47693), and Z. tritici alone from 6% to 73% between
projects that detect it. The project is the unit of analysis, because crop,
protocol and sampling design are all nested within it, so a percentage pooled
over runs would be a weighted average over a handful of independent units.

Only BioProjects whose declaration has been curated from the study's own text
are shown. 18 further projects of the cohort (344 runs) have not been curated,
so their detections are unknown rather than undeclared and an outline would
assert something the curation does not support; a further 1 (8 runs) was
curated and declared no pathogen, with a wheat pathogen called in 0 of its
runs. All are in the supplementary detection table. 5 further wheat pathogens
were called in fewer than 10 runs of the drawn projects and are not given a
row: F. avenaceum (7), O. yallundae (5), F. culmorum (3), F. graminearum (1),
A. alternata (1). Every cell, drawn or not, is in declared_cells.tsv.

Detection is presence, not activity, and a species-level call inside a densely
represented genus is a call on that genus' k-mer space; both are taken up in
04_align.

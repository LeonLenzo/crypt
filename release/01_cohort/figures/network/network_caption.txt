FULL CAPTION

How the cohort was assembled, read outward: one search, the papers it found, the
deposits those papers made, and the runs inside them. 54 papers, 31 BioProjects and
1,560 runs.

The diamond at the centre is the search. It is not a datum; it is there because
without it the graph is a dozen unconnected components that a layout drives to the
rim, and because every paper's claim to be in this figure runs through it.

Filled circles are the 28 papers whose reads are in the cohort. Hollow circles are 26
papers that passed full-text screening, report sequencing of field material, and
deposited nothing this cohort could use — mostly virome surveys reporting consensus
genomes rather than reads. A paper that deposited nothing AND ran no field sequencing
is a different kind of study and is absent entirely.

THE HOLLOW RING IS A RESULT, NOT A MARGIN. Of the papers that got this far, almost as
many deposited nothing usable as deposited something.

Blue circles are BioProjects, each surrounded by its runs: green where the run is
field-collected Triticum, hollow where it is lab material, off-host, or not RNA-seq.
network_declared.png redraws this ring by what each study declared. 1,396 of 1,560
runs are field wheat.

6 BioProjects are drawn hollow, with no sample cloud at all (125 runs). They hold no
field wheat — barley, lab material — and their runs are still counted everywhere else;
drawing their clouds spent a quarter of the canvas on samples the analysis does not
use.

EDGES ARE PRIMARY ATTRIBUTION ONLY. An edge means this paper generated this deposit.
There are 32, and every BioProject has at most one except PRJNA722004. 3 papers
deposit more than once. The figure is therefore close to a forest, which is the point:
it is what the cohort looks like once reuse is separated from generation.

Reanalysis edges are NOT drawn. 6 curated reanalyses exist and the solver can draw
them dashed (solve_network.py --reanalysis), but that is a check on the layout rather
than a page figure: it uses its own palette and its own solved positions, not these. A
further 11 paper-to-deposit links are mentions in a text nobody has adjudicated, and
are drawn nowhere.


WHAT IS NOT SHOWN

The 11 unadjudicated mentions above. "Named in a paper's text but not in its deposit
section" is not evidence of reanalysis: it is satisfied by a citation in a discussion.
Inferring reanalysis from it labelled 17 edges where 6 had ever been decided, so only
curated relations are drawn.

Node positions carry no meaning. They are read from network_nodes.tsv, a hand-editable
table seeded from the solver and then adjusted, so a node sits where it was placed:
broadly, angle is its paper's bearing and radius is the shortest the solver could
reach without an edge crossing another deposit's samples, but neither encodes a
quantity and several nodes were moved for legibility. Cloud area does track run count,
through the packing, but it is not calibrated and should not be read off.


SHORT CAPTION

The cohort as a network: 54 papers, 31 BioProjects, 1,560 runs. The centre is the
search; orange papers deposited reads the cohort uses, hollow papers are field surveys
that deposited nothing usable. Each BioProject carries its runs, green for field-
collected Triticum (1,396 of 1,560) and hollow otherwise. Edges show primary
attribution only, 32 of them; the 6 curated reanalyses are not drawn.

So the setting follows Leon's Kovaleski ruling of 2026-10-06 - field-grown tissue taken
indoors is still field-derived - but with the difference that organisms were ADDED here,
which Kovaleski did not have. setting = field (excised, lab-inoculated);
sampling_selection = inoculated, which keeps the whole project out of any prevalence
denominator.

WHY THE MOCK/TREATED SPLIT WAS NOT PURSUED. It would not change the disposition:
  - all three inocula are fungi this pipeline detects, so a hit reads the experiment
  - the material is detached, cut ("blossom end halves"), wounded and incubated humid, so any
    resident organism blooms and abundance means nothing about the field
  - mock and inoculated fruit were handled in one experiment, so spore cross-contamination
    between arms is a live risk in a shared incubator
Even a perfectly clean mock arm would be wounded detached fruit, not a plant in a field.

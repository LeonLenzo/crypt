*** ANOTHER TRAP PHRASE ***
"water-limited plants were watered only when soil moisture fell below 25% FIELD CAPACITY".
Field capacity is a soil-physics term for the water a soil holds after free drainage. It says
nothing about the setting, and a keyword search for "field" hits it. Compare the Setaria
greenhouse project, where "naturally occurring water-limited conditions" was the misleading
phrase. Both would classify as field on an abstract or a grep.

all (bmr12_fus_dry_13_d = bmr12 genotype, F. thapsinum, water-limited, 13 days after
inoculation, replicate d). The paper supplied the greenhouse, which the archive did not.

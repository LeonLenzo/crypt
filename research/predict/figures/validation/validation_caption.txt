Gene prediction recovers CDS that the reference needs and the archive does not supply. (a) Benchmark against species that DO carry a submitter annotation (n=60): k-mer recall and precision of the predicted CDS against the true CDS, by the assembly level of the target. Median recall 0.976 on complete genomes and 0.958 on contig-level assemblies (0.962 overall, precision 0.944), so fragmentation does not degrade recovery; the production targets are mostly fragmented. (b) The production run (n=135): BUSCO completeness of the predicted proteins against assembly size relative to the genus median, with marker area the contig count. Median completeness 99.5% (Q1 98.5%), against the dashed line and band, the median and interquartile range of the 1,989 DOWNLOADED annotations already in db_v4 (98.4%). 4 predictions fall below 90% and each is an assembly artefact rather than a prediction failure: three are far off their genus size, one is split over 23,880 contigs (Aspergillus sp. 22%; Vishniacozyma victoriae 59%; Pichia fermentans 76%; Puccinia brachypodii 88%). (c) Novelty of the predicted CDS against db_v4 (n=59): the fraction of predicted k-mers the database has never seen, split on whether the target's genus is already represented. Median 0.644 where the genus is present (n=22) against 0.972 where it is absent (n=37): a congener covers part of a species' k-mer space and nothing like all of it. (d) The limitation (n=53 of 60 benchmark genomes with a genome size on record): predicted CDS bases over true CDS bases against genome size, fill the prediction's precision. Over-prediction rises with genome size (r = +0.76) and precision falls with it (r = -0.72), reaching 3.91x at precision 0.51 for Blumeria graminis and 2.19x at 0.49 for Puccinia triticina. Both are wheat pathogens, which is why the wheat pathogens' own genomes are not predicted: inflating a taxon's minimizer denominator depresses the coverage of the species the chapter reports.

Not in the figure. Panel b drops 8 of 135 targets whose genus holds fewer than 3 assemblies, so no size expectation can be formed. Panel d drops 7 benchmark genomes with no genome size on record (Neurospora crassa; Saccharomycodes ludwigii; Zygosaccharomyces parabailii; Cryptococcus bacillisporus; Aspergillus bombycis; Fomitiporia mediterranea; Coniosporium apollinis). Coverage for a predicted taxon is NOT comparable to coverage for a downloaded one, and predicted CDS is a competitor in the index: it may absorb reads, and it never supports a positive call about the species it came from.

WHAT THIS FIGURE SHOWS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Two stacked bar charts comparing co-infection characteristics between the two
search modes (MAL and HAL), using biosample-representative confirmed runs.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PANEL A — Co-infection flag breakdown
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
X: mode (MAL, HAL). Y: proportion of all BioSamples.
Colours:
  Light grey (#dfe6e9) — single (no co-infection detected)
  Orange     (#e67e22) — multi_species (≥2 pathogens, same kingdom)
  Red        (#c0392b) — multi_kingdom (pathogens from ≥2 kingdoms)

co_infection_flag priority in pipeline: multi_kingdom > multi_species > single.

Key comparison: MAL vs HAL proportion of multi_species and multi_kingdom
detections. MAL is expected to have more co-infections because the library
organism is a pathogen (already infected plant), making co-infection
biologically more likely in field samples. HAL covers a broader host set
with more controlled-setting studies (lower background rate expected).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PANEL B — Secondary kingdom composition
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Restricted to co-infected BioSamples (co_infection_flag ≠ "single") with
a non-empty secondary_kingdoms field.
X: mode (MAL, HAL). Y: proportion of secondary detections.
Colours: Fungi=#d35400, Viruses=#c0392b, Bacteria=#2980b9,
         Oomycota=#8e44ad, Nematoda=#27ae60, Unknown=#95a5a6

Secondary kingdom is parsed from stat_pathogens in runs.tsv; each BioSample
can contribute multiple kingdoms if it has multi-kingdom co-infection.
Labels shown for segments ≥ 3%.

Key comparison: which kingdoms dominate secondary detections in each mode.
Fungi are expected to dominate in MAL (pathogen-as-library studies are
heavily fungal-biased because PHI-base fungi are well-represented). Virus
proportion may differ between modes because HAL captures host transcriptome
studies where plant viruses are more likely to be an unreported co-infector.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CAVEATS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Kingdom proportions reflect STAT detection thresholds, not true biological
  prevalence: Fungi ≥1%, Viruses ≥10%, Bacteria ≥5%, Oomycota ≥0.5%,
  Nematoda ≥1%. The virus threshold is deliberately high to reduce false
  positives; virus secondary detections are therefore underrepresented
  relative to their likely true prevalence.
- same_genus_secondary filter NOT applied here — includes all co-infections
  including same-genus Puccinia pairs. For the conservative estimate, see
  the study design figures and guild network.
- "Unknown" secondary kingdom = STAT pathogen name not resolvable to a
  PHI-base taxid or not in the ICTV plant virus list.

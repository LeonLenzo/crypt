Figure X. Evidence-gated funnel for cryptic co-infection detection across SRA RNA-seq
BioSamples.

Sankey diagram showing the progressive classification and co-infection detection of 9,002
non-redundant BioSamples (one representative RNA-seq run per biological sample) derived from
NCBI SRA. Upstream pipeline: 608,368 runs were screened using NCBI STAT pre-computed k-mer
taxonomy; 10,995 passed the in-planta confirmation gate (MAL mode: ≥1% Viridiplantae reads;
HAL mode: ≥1% PHI-base eukaryotic pathogen detected), and were deduplicated to 9,002
BioSamples by retaining the highest-analysed run per BioSample.

The diagram is structured as a five-column funnel in which BioSamples that cannot be
classified at each stage terminate at that column (grey nodes) and do not proceed further.
Only BioSamples classifiable at all three intermediate stages reach co-infection detection.

(1) All 9,002 BioSamples enter from the left. (2) Evidence quality: BioSamples are divided
by the availability of supporting literature. Those whose parent BioProject has a linked
publication (5,914; 65.7%) or BioSample XML metadata (geo_loc_name, tissue, or
collection_date; 2,543; 28.2%) proceed; the 545 (6.1%) with neither terminate here.
(3) Study setting: the remaining 8,457 BioSamples are classified by a GPT-4o-mini LLM
applied to BioProject title, description, PMC abstract and methods text. BioSamples whose
BioProject was classified as field-collected (1,219; 13.5%) or controlled conditions
(4,655; 51.7%) proceed; 2,583 (28.7%) for which setting could not be determined terminate
here. Setting classification required explicit field collection language in the text;
geographic metadata (geo_loc_name) alone was not considered sufficient evidence. (4) Study
design: within each setting, BioSamples are further divided by the declared experimental
focus, also LLM-classified: biotic stress (single declared pathogen, co-infection experiment,
or surveillance; field 877; controlled 4,009), abiotic stress (drought, heat, salinity or
cold; field 116; controlled 118), and no declared stress (pure host transcriptomics or
development; field 226; controlled 523). BioSamples whose design could not be determined (5)
terminate here. (5) Co-infection status: the 5,869 fully classified BioSamples are separated
by STAT k-mer detection into co-infected and not co-infected, shown separately for field and
controlled settings. Co-infection is restricted to eukaryotic plant pathogens only; bacteria
and viruses are excluded because polyA+ RNA-seq library preparation strongly depletes
bacterial mRNA (no polyA tails) and most plant virus families (tospoviruses, tobamoviruses,
cucumoviruses, luteoviruses) also lack polyA tails. A co-infection is flagged when one or
more fungi, oomycetes, or nematodes from the PHI-base plant pathogen reference are detected
above kingdom-specific thresholds (fungi ≥0.5%, oomycetes ≥0.5%, nematodes ≥1%) in addition
to the declared primary organism.

Node width is proportional to BioSample count. Co-infection rates are shown as percentages
within each terminal co-infection node. Field-collected BioSamples show consistently higher
co-infection prevalence than controlled-condition samples across all study design categories
(overall 22.7% vs 11.4%). Within field samples, biotic stress studies show the highest rate
(24.4%), followed by no-stress host studies (19.0%) and abiotic stress (17.2%). Within
controlled samples, abiotic stress studies show the highest rate (26.3%), followed by no-
stress host studies (12.8%) and biotic stress studies (10.8%). Co-infection detection used
NCBI STAT pre-computed k-mer taxonomy against the PHI-base eukaryotic plant pathogen
reference (fungi, oomycetes, and nematodes; ~8,250 total taxids after strain expansion).

Colour scheme: blue tones indicate evidence quality (dark blue: with publication; mid blue:
metadata only); green tones indicate field-setting nodes and flows (dark to light: biotic,
abiotic, no stress); blue-grey tones indicate controlled-setting nodes and flows; grey
indicates terminal (unclassified) nodes and no-co-infection flows; orange indicates
co-infection detection.

Reference scope of db_v4. Circular cladogram of the 1,544 fungal species that carry a gene model and can therefore enter a CDS-only index, drawn on the NCBI taxonomy held in holobase: topology is taxonomic, branch lengths are not evolutionary distance, and radius encodes taxonomic RANK (kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, species) rather than any measure of divergence. Tip colour gives the route that brought the species into scope, taking the strongest evidence where several apply (host (plant) 6, pathogen recorded on wheat, rice, maize or sorghum 121, plant pathogen, not recorded on those crops 694, host associate 67, plant associate 508, congener 148); congeners are grey because they are present to give the classifier a competitor and are not reportable as detections. The radial bar is the number of ANNOTATED assemblies the species holds on a log10 scale, 4,283 in total; the 8,861 further assemblies these species hold without a gene model are not drawn, because a CDS-only index cannot use them. Bars are wedges that tile their angular slot exactly rather than fixed-width lines, so no tip overdraws its neighbour at any radius. Known plant pathogens are split by whether they are recorded on one of the four cohort cereals (121 of them, 8% of all tips): a pathogen detected on a crop it is already recorded from is an expected finding, while one detected on a crop it has never been recorded from is a potential new host association. The split is a LABEL and not a filter: scoping the database by the cohort's own hosts is forbidden, because the study exists to find UNREPORTED associations and such a filter would make them undetectable by construction. Absence of a record is NOT evidence that a fungus cannot infect a cereal, because the source holds only what has been observed and written down; the species without one are precisely the population in which an unreported association could be found. A further 2,138 species have at least one assembly but no gene model anywhere and are not shown, because a CDS-only index cannot hold them until gene prediction has run; they are the worklist, not a rejection. Classes with at least 40 species are labelled.

Values are relative abundance WITHIN each taxonomic level, summing to ~1 at class rank and
0.62-0.97 at species rank (the remainder is reads not resolved to a retained species). Their
retention filter was >=25 reads in >=1/3 of a dataset's samples.

Why these are here rather than in 03_kraken: studies/ is the module's drop zone for published
papers and supplements. They are consumed by `03_kraken/01_search/scope_residents.py`, which
reaches them through `_paths.COLVIN_DATA`. Soybean is not used (not a cohort host).

**Their abundances are a Kraken2 + Bracken + core_nt output, not ground truth.** See the
"not ground truth" section of `03_kraken/01_search/README.md` before quoting any number.

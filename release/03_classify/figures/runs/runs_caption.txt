FULL CAPTION

Four of the columns in the panel above, drawn in full. Each point is one taxon
Kraken2 assigned reads to in that run, placed by the two quantities the call
is made on: redundancy available on the x axis, the reads the library could
have spent on that taxon, and coverage observed on the y, the share of its
k-mer space actually seen. Both are divided by the taxon's OWN minimizer total
in db2, which is what makes the null one curve for every taxon rather than one
per taxon. Axes are log scales labelled as the exponent. Filled points are
calls, hollow points are not; 18 of 830 candidates across the four runs are
called.

Three lines, and together they are the whole decision. The solid curve is the
null, the coverage that reads spread uniformly would give at that depth. The
dotted line is the coverage floor at 2%, anchored on the off-host null. The
dashed curve is the abundance floor, 0.05 of the null, which bends down at low
depth because a shallow library could not have reached 5% whatever was
present. The two cross at redundancy 0.51: above it the dashed curve binds,
below it the dotted line does, and no point is ever judged by both.

The four runs are a ladder through the control set, and each was chosen with
its expectation written down first, in data/inputs/panel_runs.tsv, so the
panels are a test rather than an illustration:

  1. pure fungal (spores) (SRR6043971, spores): 68 candidates, 2 called \u2014 Puccinia striiformis, Triticum aestivum
  2. controlled infection (ERR1607743, infected_tissue): 143 candidates, 4 called \u2014 Cladosporium cladosporioides, Cladosporium inversicolor, Puccinia striiformis, Triticum aestivum
  3. field sample (ERR15005445, field-collected): 299 candidates, 11 called \u2014 Alternaria triticimaculans, Cladosporium allicinum, Dioszegia hungarica, Parastagonospora nodorum, Puccinia graminis, Puccinia striiformis, Puccinia triticina, Pyrenophora tritici-repentis, Sporobolomyces roseus, Triticum aestivum, Zymoseptoria tritici
  4. host only (SRR14239691, host_only): 320 candidates, 1 called \u2014 Triticum aestivum

Read left to right they say what a column of the panel above can contain. A
spore library has one organism in it and the method finds one. A glasshouse
plant is not sterile, and the Cladosporium in the second panel is the method
behaving correctly rather than failing. A field sample carries a crowd. And an
uninoculated plant carries no fungus at all, which is the only panel where the
right answer is nothing and the floors have to decline everything above the
rejected cloud \u2014 the host is still called there, because the host is
ranked and not thresholded.

The rejected cloud is most of each panel and is deliberately unlabelled. It
has no identity worth reading: what it shows is that the floors refuse far
more than they admit, and that the refusals lie along the null rather than
scattered, which is what cross-mapping from a large reference looks like.
